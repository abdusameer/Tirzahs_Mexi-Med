FAQ

Before you walk up.

Is there parking?

Yes, a small private lot, plus bike parking. The lot fills up at lunch, so street parking is usually easier.

Can I eat there?

Yes. There's a patio with tables in the sun, and food for here comes out on real plates.

What can vegetarians get?

Ask for falafel or jackfruit in place of meat. Yelp lists vegan options and limited vegetarian options.

Do you deliver?

Yes. Order takeout or delivery through Yelp, or find Tirzahs on DoorDash.

Can I bring my dog?

Yes, dogs are allowed.

Do you cater?

Yes. Call (323) 612-6062 to talk about catering.

Why does my food take a few minutes?

It's made to order, and the taco tortillas are handmade. The portions are big, too.
